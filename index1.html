Match Rules

  1. No leg umpire required. Umpires to call LBW only if clear.
  2. Above shoulder is a no ball; free hit next ball.
  3. Foot on or before the crease while bowling = legal.
  4. Batsman may ground bat behind crease to save run out if part of bat over crease.
  5. Each match fixed overs: recommend 6, 8 or 10 overs per side.
  6. Super over rules / tie-breaker: 2 balls each.
