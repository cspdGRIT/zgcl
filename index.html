📜 GULLY CRICKET TOURNAMENT RULEBOOK 2025

1. Tournament Format

Teams & Matches
  • Total 6 teams will participate.
  • Each team plays against every other team once.
  • Matches are 6 overs per side, and each fixture consists of a best of 3 games.
Points System & Qualification
  • Win → 2 points | Loss → 0 points.
  • Top 2 teams based on points reach the Final.
  • In case of equal points, the team with higher total runs scored advances.

2. Umpiring & Conduct

Umpires
  • Surya, Sandeep, Sunil, etc will officiate as umpires for most matches.
  • No leg umpire required.
  • Third umpires may be there as group of match observers.
  • Their decision is final and binding.
Spirit of the Game
  • Sledging is allowed in good spirit — fun, not personal or abusive.
  • Disputes must be handled amicably by team captains and umpires.

3. Bowling Rules

Bowling Action
  • Foot must land on or before the crease.
  • Above shoulder height delivery = No Ball.
  • One bowler can bowl a maximum of 2 overs.
  • Bowlers who are unwilling can skip bowling — the captain must adjust accordingly.
Wides & No Balls
  • Wide ball = 1 run + re-bowl.
  • No ball = 1 run + free hit.

4. Batting & Running Rules

  • Batsman only needs to reach the crease line, not crash into the wall.
  • Last man batting is allowed.
  • Captains must ensure everyone gets a chance to bat across matches.
  • Run outs apply on both ends. Caught & Bowled, Run Out, Bowled, Stumped — all valid as usual.
  • One-step catches are allowed. If fielder touches boundary while holding ball, it is a boundary.
  • If the ball goes directly outside the boundary (beyond the Zenith compound, etc.), batsman is OUT.

5. Fielding Rules

  • Max 2 fielders on each side of the pitch (leg/off). Max 3 by mutual agreement.
  • Ball hitting a fielder in contact with the boundary = boundary.
  • Over-boundary catch not allowed.

6. Player Substitutions

  • If a player cannot attend, the captain may find a substitute from another team with opposing captain's agreement.

7. Match Arrangements

  • All balls must be of uniform standard. Bats/stumps/markers provided by organizers.
  • ORS/Water to be provided near grass side.

8. Bonus Suggestions

  • Fair Play Award, Best Catch, Best Bowler, Best Batter in Finals.
  • Man of the Match for each game.
  • Photo/video coverage encouraged!
