📋 Tournament Rules

⚾ Umpiring & General Play

  • Everyone in the group must umpire as many matches as possible
  • No leg umpire needed
  • Batsman reaching crease (even touching) counts as safe on runs—no crashing into walls
  • Batsmen limited to max 1 run to prevent injuries/wall crashes
  • Keep non-striker/runner
  • Last single batsman allowed
  • Toss required for all best-of-3 games (finals)

🎾 Bowling Rules

  • No-ball: Above shoulder height
  • Bowler's foot on or before crease
  • Wide: 1 run + re-bowl
  • Balls: Brand new Wilson—same standard for all matches

🏃 Fielding & Boundaries

  • Max 3 fielders per side (one side only)
  • Catches: One-step allowed; current boundary rules apply
  • Fours: Fielder touches boundary with ball = 4; touches wall = 4
  • No boundary if ball hits cement bags, deflects, and crosses
  • Captain rotates fielders so everyone fields/bowls (6 on field max at a time)
  • Outs: Ball beyond boundary direct (even outside Zenith) = out; run-outs both sides

🔄 Bowling Rotations (Player-Dependent)

  • 6 players: All bowl 1 over; 1 bowler does extra (bowling team's choice). Non-bowler? Batting team picks replacement.
  • 7 players: All bowl 1 over; 1 extra over (bowling team choice). Non-bowler? Batting team picks. Impact bowler gets the extra.

🏆 Tournament Format

  • League: All 5 teams play each other (1 game/match, 7 overs/side) = 10 total league matches
  • Captain duty: Ensure everyone bats across matches if not all bat in one
  • Tiebreaker: Same points? Higher team = more total runs across league games
  • Playoffs: Top 4 teams qualify
    • Qualifier 1: T1 vs T2 (winner to Finals)
    • Eliminator: T3 vs T4 (loser eliminated)
    • Qualifier 2: Loser of Q1 vs Winner of Eliminator (winner to Finals)
  • Finals: Best-of-3
