📜 Gully Cricket Rules 2025

1. Tournament Format

Teams & Matches
  • 6 teams participate
  • Round-robin format - everyone plays everyone
  • 6 overs per side, best of 3 per fixture
Points System
  • Win = 2 points, Loss = 0 points
  • Top 2 teams reach the final
  • Tiebreaker: Higher total runs scored

2. Bowling Rules

  • Foot must land before crease
  • Above shoulder = No Ball
  • Max 2 overs per bowler
  • Wide ball = 1 run + re-bowl
  • No ball = 1 run + free hit

3. Batting Rules

  • Reach crease line to complete run
  • Last man batting allowed
  • Everyone gets a chance to bat
  • Direct hit outside compound = OUT

4. Fielding Rules

  • Max 2 fielders each side (leg/off)
  • One-step catches allowed
  • Fielder touching boundary with ball = boundary

5. Spirit of the Game

  • Sledging allowed in good spirit
  • No personal or abusive comments
  • Umpire's decision is final
  • Play fair, have fun! 🏏
